Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Piggy does not collect, sell or share your personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Data stored on your device

  • Open tabs, bookmarks, browsing history, downloads and per-site settings are stored only on your device. You can clear history and website data in Settings.
  • Private tabs keep no history, and their cookies and website data are discarded when they close.

Websites you visit

Pages you open are loaded directly from those websites, which may collect data under their own privacy policies. Searches typed in the address bar are sent to the search engine you choose (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Naver).

Camera, microphone and location

Piggy only uses these when a website asks and you allow it. Piggy itself does not record or store this data.

Face ID / Touch ID

Used only to unlock private tabs, through Apple's system authentication. Piggy never receives your biometric data.

한국어 요약

Piggy는 개인정보를 수집·판매·공유하지 않습니다. 계정, 분석 도구, 광고가 없습니다. 탭·즐겨찾기·방문 기록·다운로드는 기기에만 저장됩니다. 방문한 웹사이트와 선택한 검색 엔진에는 각자의 개인정보 처리방침이 적용됩니다. 카메라·마이크·위치는 웹사이트가 요청하고 사용자가 허용할 때만 사용됩니다.
